# CleanConnect 1.1.0 — App Store Release Notes

## What's New

- Focused mission: a simpler three-tab experience — Events, Feed, Profile — so it's faster to find a cleanup near you and post one of your own.
- Document your cleanup with a video link. Drop a YouTube Short, Instagram Reel, or any video URL — no more uploading photos to prove your work.
- Tip creators directly through your own UPI app or PayPal. We never touch the money — your tip goes straight from your account to theirs.
- Pitch in at community events with one tap. See who's joining, add a safety note, and link up via WhatsApp.
