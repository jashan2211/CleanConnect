CleanConnect 2.0 is a full rebuild of the app.

HOW TO TEST
• No login is needed to browse: the Home feed, Map, Drives, Leaders and public profiles all work as a guest.
• To post, tap "Report a spot" (camera button) and choose "Sign in with Apple". Any Apple ID works; no demo account is needed. Google sign-in is also offered.
• Report flow: add a photo from the library or camera, pick a category and size, confirm the location and tap "Post report".
• Open any spot, tap "Already cleaned", add an after photo and post. The spot turns green and a before/after slider appears.
• Drives tab: "Organise a cleanup drive" creates an event others can join.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)
• Users accept the Terms and Community Guidelines (zero tolerance for objectionable content) before signing in.
• Every spot, drive, comment and profile has "Report content" and "Block" in its ⋯ menu. Blocked users' content disappears immediately.
• Content reported by 3 different people is hidden automatically; the developer reviews all reports within 24 hours.

PAYMENTS (Guideline 3.2.1(vii))
• The app sells nothing and has no in-app purchases.
• Users can optionally add their UPI ID. Others can open their own UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, etc.) to send a gift directly to that person. It is optional, 100% goes to the receiver, the app never handles money and nothing in the app is unlocked by paying.

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
• You tab → gear icon → Settings → Delete account. This removes the profile and the user's content and revokes the Sign in with Apple token.

PERMISSIONS
• Location (when in use): pins reports and shows nearby spots. Camera: photographing spots. Photo library add: only when the user saves a share card.
