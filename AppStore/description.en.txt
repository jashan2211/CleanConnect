Report garbage spots · Join cleanup drives · Earn Swachh Points

CleanConnect is the community app for cleaner streets, lakes and parks across India. Spot a mess, put it on the map, clean it up with your neighbours and show the world the before & after.

SPOT IT. SNAP IT.
See a garbage dump, a blocked drain, plastic by the lake or paan-stained walls? Take a photo, pick what it is and post it in about 10 seconds. Your report is pinned on a live map that everyone in your city can see. Neighbours tap "Still dirty" to confirm it, so the worst spots rise to the top.

CLEAN IT. PROVE IT.
Tap "I'll clean this", get to work and add the after photo. Your before/after slider goes into the feed for all of India to see, and makes a great WhatsApp status. Other people can verify the cleanup, so the map stays honest.

RALLY YOUR MOHALLA
Start a cleanup drive in a minute: pick a meeting point, set a time, list what to bring and share the invite to your WhatsApp groups. Volunteers RSVP in one tap, get a reminder the day before and can add it to their calendar.

ESCALATE TO THE AUTHORITIES
Some piles need a truck, not a pair of gloves. CleanConnect drafts a polite complaint with the photo, a map link and the number of residents who confirmed the problem, ready to send to your municipal corporation, ward office or MLA.

EARN SWACHH POINTS
Earn points for reporting, cleaning, verifying and joining drives. Level up from Safai Starter to Swachh Superstar, unlock badges, keep a daily streak and climb the leaderboards for your city, your state and all of India.

SUPPORT CLEANERS DIRECTLY
Cleaners and drive organisers can add their UPI ID to their profile. If you'd like to say thanks or chip in for gloves and bags, you pay them directly from your own UPI app. It's completely optional, 100% goes to the person you're supporting, and CleanConnect never handles the money or takes a cut.

MADE FOR INDIA
• English and Hindi
• Works in every city, town and village
• Share cards made for WhatsApp and Instagram
• Browse without an account; sign in with Apple or Google to post
• Free, with no ads

SAFE AND RESPECTFUL
Report or block anyone in a tap. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically, and every report is reviewed within 24 hours. When you clean, please stay safe: wear gloves, go with others, keep away from traffic and never handle needles or medical waste.

Every clean street starts with one photo. Let's clean India together.
