CleanConnect

Clean India Together 🇮🇳

CleanConnect is on the App Store Report garbage · Clean up · Free
Download on theApp Store

Spot a garbage dump? Snap it and pin it on your city's live map. Claim it, clean it and share the before & after. Rally your mohalla for cleanup drives, earn Swachh Points and climb the leaderboards for your city, your state and all of India.

Everything your street needs to get clean

📸 Report in 10 seconds

Photograph a garbage dump, blocked drain, plastic litter or paan-stained wall, pick what it is and post. It goes straight onto a live map everyone in your city can see.

✨ Before & after

Claim a spot, clean it and add the after photo. Your before/after slider lands in the feed, and other people can verify the cleanup so the map stays honest.

🧹 Cleanup drives

Organise a drive in a minute: meeting point, time, what to bring. Share the invite on WhatsApp, get RSVPs and send everyone a reminder the day before.

🏛️ Escalate to the authorities

For piles that need a truck, CleanConnect drafts a polite complaint with the photo, a map link and how many residents confirmed it, ready for your municipal corporation.

🏆 Swachh Points & leaderboards

Earn points for reporting, cleaning, verifying and joining drives. Level up from Safai Starter to Swachh Superstar, keep a streak and top your city's weekly board.

🙏 Support cleaners directly

Cleaners can add a UPI ID. Thank them straight from your own UPI app: it's optional, 100% goes to them, and CleanConnect never touches the money.

Questions

Is CleanConnect free?

Yes. It's free to download and use, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to browse the map, feed, drives and leaderboards. Sign in with Apple or Google when you want to report, clean, comment or join a drive.

How does UPI support work?

People can add their UPI ID to their profile. If you want to thank a cleaner or chip in for a drive's supplies, you pay them directly from your own UPI app. It's optional, 100% goes to them, and CleanConnect never handles money or takes a cut.

Is it available in Hindi?

Yes. The app follows your iPhone's language and is fully available in English and Hindi.

Which iPhones does it run on?

iOS 18 or later, on iPhone and iPad.
