## Frequently Asked Questions

### Account & Sign-In

**How do I create an account?**
Download CleanConnect and sign in using your Apple ID or Google account. We use secure authentication to protect your information.

**How do I sign out?**
Go to Profile > Settings > Sign Out.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Profile > Settings > Account Settings > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

**Why can't I sign in?**
- Ensure you have a stable internet connection
- Try signing in with the same method you used to create your account (Apple or Google)
- If issues persist, contact us at info@thebighead.ca

### Posts & Content

**How do I create a post?**
Tap the "+" button on the home screen, add photos of your cleanup activity, write a description, and share!

**What should I post?**
- Before and after photos of cleanup areas
- Progress updates from your cleanup activities
- Tips and techniques for effective cleanup
- Photos from gatherings and events

**Why was my post removed?**
Posts may be removed if they violate our content guidelines, including:
- Inappropriate or offensive content
- Spam or misleading information
- Content unrelated to environmental cleanup
- Copyright violations

**Can I edit or delete my posts?**
Yes! Tap on your post and select Edit or Delete from the options menu.

---

### Tips & Payments

**How do I send a tip?**
On any post, tap the "Tip" button, select an amount (₹10 - ₹10,000), and complete payment through Stripe.

**How do I receive tips?**
1. Go to Profile > Settings > Payment Settings
2. Complete Stripe Connect onboarding
3. Once verified, you can receive tips from other users

**What is the platform fee?**
CleanConnect retains 7% of each tip as a service fee. Creators receive 93% of all tips.

**How long until I receive my tip money?**
Tips are processed through Stripe and typically arrive in your connected bank account within 2-7 business days, depending on your bank.

**Are tips refundable?**
Tips are non-refundable once processed. Please review before confirming your tip.

**I didn't receive my tip. What should I do?**
Contact us at info@thebighead.ca with the following information:
- Your username
- Date and amount of the tip
- Any error messages you received

### Gatherings & Events

**How do I create a gathering?**
Go to Gatherings > Create Event. Fill in the details including location, date, time, and description. Other users can then discover and join your event.

**How do I join a gathering?**
Browse gatherings in your area, tap on one you're interested in, and tap "Join." You'll receive notifications about event updates.

**Is CleanConnect responsible for event safety?**
No. CleanConnect facilitates connections but is not responsible for the safety of in-person events. Please exercise caution, follow local guidelines, and prioritize your personal safety.

**Can I cancel a gathering I created?**
Yes. Go to your gathering, tap Edit, and select Cancel Event. All participants will be notified.

### Points & Badges

**How do I earn points?**
- Creating posts: 50 points
- Organizing events: 100 points
- Attending events: 25 points
- Receiving tips: 1 point per ₹10

**What are badges?**
Badges are achievements that recognize your contributions:
- Eco Scout (Level 1)
- Green Guardian (Level 5)
- Planet Protector (Level 10)
- And many more!

**How do leaderboards work?**
Leaderboards rank users by total points within their state and nationally. Keep contributing to climb the ranks!

### Technical Issues

**The app is crashing. What should I do?**
1. Force close the app and reopen it
2. Check for app updates in the App Store
3. Restart your device
4. Reinstall the app (your data is saved in the cloud)
5. If issues persist, contact us with your device model and iOS version

**Photos won't upload. What's wrong?**
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure CleanConnect has permission to access your photos (Settings > CleanConnect > Photos)
- Try uploading smaller images (max 10MB per image)
- Make sure you have sufficient storage on your device

**I'm not receiving notifications.**
- Check that notifications are enabled (Settings > CleanConnect > Notifications)
- Ensure Do Not Disturb is not enabled
- Check that you haven't muted specific notification types in the app

### Privacy & Security

**Is my data secure?**
Yes. We use industry-standard security measures including:
- Encrypted data transmission
- Secure authentication via Apple/Google
- Firebase security rules
- No storage of complete payment card details

**How can I see what data you have about me?**
Contact us at info@thebighead.ca to request a copy of your data.

**How do I report a user or content?**
Tap the three-dot menu on any post or profile and select "Report." Choose the reason and submit. We review all reports within 24 hours.
